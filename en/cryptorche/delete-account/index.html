Delete my Cryptorche account

Last updated : November 11, 2026

You can delete your Cryptorche account and associated data at any time, free of charge. App: Cryptorche, developer: Marega Studio.

In the game (recommended)

  1. Open Cryptorche, then Settings → Delete account.
  2. Confirm twice (double confirmation: the action is permanent).

The account is deleted on the server immediately; any backup copies are purged within 30 days. Unspent gems are lost.

Without the game (uninstalled or inaccessible)

Send us your request with the account ID shown in the game (Settings → Account). Without it, we cannot locate an anonymous account.

Send the request by email

Or write to lufygate@gmail.com with the subject "Cryptorche account deletion". We may ask for simple proof that the account is yours. We process requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The account (ID, device secret);
  • the online save: gem balance, inventory, quests, pass, spending cap, match summaries;
  • technical logs tied to the account (automatically purged after 12 months).

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records (transaction ID, amount, date) are kept 10 years to meet our statutory accounting obligations (French Commercial Code, art. L123-22), unlinked from your game profile once the account is deleted.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase data under its own rules.

Minors and other rights

A parent or legal guardian may request deletion of a child's account the same way. Other rights (access, portability...): see the privacy policy.
