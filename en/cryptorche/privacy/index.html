Summary

Cryptorche is a free mobile game with no ads. The offline version sends nothing. The version with online services automatically creates an anonymous account: we never ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and never access your contacts.

We use no advertising, no analytics, no commercial profiling, and we do not sell data. Game data is sent over HTTPS only.

Recipients and processors

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Paris (France) region: hosts the game server and logs.
  • Google (Google Play, Google Payments): app distribution and payment. Google handles payment and gives us a purchase record, never your card details. Google's own policies apply.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this static website and DNS. It sees your IP address when you visit these pages; no cookie is set.
  • OVH: domain name registrar.

Transfers outside the European Union

The game server is in the European Union. Google and Cloudflare may process data in the United States; such transfers rely on the Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses (GDPR art. 44 to 46).

Cookies and trackers

The game uses no cookie and no advertising ID. The account ID and device secret, strictly necessary for the account to work, are stored encrypted on your device. This website uses no cookie, no analytics and no external script.

Minors

Under 15 (France), a parent or legal guardian's consent is required to use an online service. Purchases are for adults or minors authorised by their guardian. A parent may request deletion of their child's account through the account deletion page.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR art. 15 to 22). To delete your account, follow the dedicated page. For other rights, email {{EDITEUR_EMAIL}} with your account ID; we reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If we add analytics or crash reporting, this page will be updated and name the provider before it is switched on.
